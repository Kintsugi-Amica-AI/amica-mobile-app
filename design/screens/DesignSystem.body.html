Rules that hold it together

One terracotta fill per screen. It means emergency and nothing else — never a decorative accent.

Sage means “you are fine”. Gold means “something needs you”. Never mix them in one row.

Nothing destructive on a single tap. Sending an alert takes a 2s hold; standing one down takes a PIN.

No blur behind text, no glow. Depth comes from a warm shadow and a hairline, so contrast survives sunlight.

Display speaks, text works. Bricolage Grotesque only for the sentence that addresses her; everything operational is Public Sans. Both are bundled in the app, not fetched — Amica has to render with no network.
